Privacy Policy for Chroma Blitz

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Chroma Blitz is a reflex game developed by Alejandro Puerta (Alpuemed). This policy explains what data is collected when using the app and for what purpose.

1. Data we collect

Local ranking (on your device)

  • The level reached in each game and the date/time are stored only in your device's local storage.
  • This data is not sent to any of our servers nor shared with third parties. It is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Google Play Games Services (global ranking, optional)

  • If you sign in with your Google Play Games account, the app sends the level reached to the Google Play Games leaderboard, and shows your public Play Games profile name next to your rank.
  • This service is managed by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy to learn how it handles that data.
  • If you don't sign in, the app still works normally with the local ranking.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Chroma Blitz shows full-screen (interstitial) ads after finishing a game, managed by Google AdMob.
  • Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, technical device information, an approximate location (based on IP), and ad interaction data, in order to show and measure ads.
  • More info: how Google uses data when apps use Google's services.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, in accordance with applicable regulations.

Chroma Blitz has no account system of its own and does not directly ask you for your name, email address, or any other contact information.

2. Children

Chroma Blitz is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what is described in this policy (which applies equally to all users).

3. Your choices

  • You can play without signing in to Google Play Games; the local ranking will keep working normally.
  • You can manage personalized ad permissions from your Google account's ad settings (adssettings.google.com).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally saved data (ranking, audio/language preferences).

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or the services it uses change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
