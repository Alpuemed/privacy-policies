Privacy Policy for Chroma Blitz

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Chroma Blitz is a reflex game developed by Alejandro Puerta (Alpuemed). This policy describes information that may be processed when you use the app, including through Google Play Games Services, Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

1. Data we collect

The birth-year question and controls below apply to versions that include them. Earlier versions may behave differently; update the app when the new version is available.

Local data

  • The level and date/time of each game, up to 50 top local scores, and audio/language settings are stored in app storage. The app asks for your birth year, not your full birth date. The year is stored only on this device in a separate preferences file excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. The app derives an adult, minor or unknown treatment when it opens; it does not send the year to us or Google.
  • Other app preferences and the local ranking may be backed up or restored by Android according to your device and account settings. An old age-category setting may remain after an update but is not used to authorize services.

Google Play Games Services

  • After a valid birth year is saved, the app may automatically try to sign in to Google Play Games once per app session, for adults and minors. Without a saved valid year it does not attempt automatic sign-in. You can cancel sign-in and continue playing locally, or choose CONNECT manually. If signed in, scores and achievements may be sent to Google and your Play Games name and rank may appear in the ranking.
  • Google manages this service. See Google Play Games data information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The app may request interstitial ads after a game and offer optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads appear only if you choose them; rewards are granted only after confirmed completion. Availability depends on consent, inventory and the current age treatment.
  • For a minor or unknown treatment, the app requests child-directed treatment, a G content rating and non-personalized ads. For an adult treatment, it refreshes UMP consent information at each app opening and after a change to adult, presents a form when required, and requests ads only after the SDK currently permits them. Failed consent updates block ads until a safe retry. Age treatment does not prevent Play Games or Google SDKs from processing data under their own policies.
  • Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process advertising or device identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions and diagnostics depending on services and settings. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Refusing personalized ads does not necessarily stop all processing or non-personalized ads.

2. Children

Chroma Blitz is intended for people aged 6 and older. Birth year is used as a conservative local classification: the app treats a person as an adult from the calendar year in which they turn 19. A missing, invalid or unreadable year receives unknown protected treatment. This is not legal age verification. Minors can play, use the local ranking and attempt Play Games sign-in after saving a valid year. Ads for minor and unknown treatments use the protected settings described above.

3. Your choices

  • You can correct your birth year in Settings. Canceling the first question lets you play with unknown treatment and asks again at the next opening. Canceling a correction keeps the saved year.
  • You can cancel Google Play Games sign-in and continue locally. Google account settings control information already sent to its services.
  • When applicable, review or change advertising consent in Settings → Privacy options; you can also manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can clear app data or uninstall to remove the locally stored birth year and other local data. The birth year is excluded from Android backup and transfer; other app data may be restored, and Play Games scores may return after sign-in. Google controls retention and deletion of data sent to its services under its policies. See Google Play's data deletion guidance.

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or the services it uses change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
