Privacy Policy for Chroma Blitz

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Chroma Blitz is a reflex game developed by Alejandro Puerta (Alpuemed). This policy describes information that may be processed when you use the app, including through Google Play Games Services, Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

1. Data we collect

The age-category selector, the advertising controls described below and the statement that the app does not request an exact birth date apply to versions that include that selector. Earlier versions may not include these controls; their advertising and other services follow the included SDK behavior and Google settings. Update to a version with the selector when one is available to you.

Local ranking and age category (app storage)

  • The level reached in each game and its date/time are stored in the app's local storage. The age category (adult, minor or unknown) is also stored on the device; the app does not ask for or store an exact date of birth.
  • Android may back up or restore some app data depending on your device and account settings. If you use Google Play Games, ranking and achievements may also be sent to that service, as described below.

Google Play Games Services (ranking and achievements)

  • After you choose an age category, the app may automatically attempt to sign in to Google Play Games. If sign-in succeeds, the app can send your level to its leaderboard and achievements to Google Play Games, and show your public Play Games profile name with your rank. You can cancel or not complete sign-in and continue local play.
  • This service is managed by Google. See Google Play Games data information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • For the adult profile, the app may request interstitial ads after a game and may offer optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are shown only after the player chooses that option; a reward is granted only when the ad reports completion.
  • In versions that include the age category selector, ad requests and formats are blocked for the minor and unknown profiles. This does not mean that no information is processed by other services, including Play Games.
  • Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process information needed to provide, manage, measure and protect advertising and consent choices. This may include advertising or device identifiers, technical device information, approximate location based on IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics; the exact information depends on services and settings in use. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, how Google uses data when apps use Google's services and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • For the adult profile, when the ad service is initialized, UMP checks whether consent information must be updated and shows a form when required by the configured message and applicable region (including the EEA, UK and Switzerland). Ad requests are made only when the SDK reports that they are allowed. Refusing personalized ads does not necessarily stop all data processing or non-personalized ads.

Chroma Blitz has no account system of its own and does not ask for an exact birth date. Google Play Games sign-in and other Google services are separate services and may process information under Google's policies.

2. Children

Chroma Blitz is intended for people aged 6 and older. The app asks users to choose an adult (18+), minor (under 18) or unknown age category; it does not ask for an exact age or birth date. The minor and unknown profiles use protected ad settings and block ad requests and formats. Play Games may still attempt sign-in after any age category is chosen, and may process data if sign-in succeeds, as described above.

3. Your choices

  • You can cancel or decline the Google Play Games sign-in attempt and continue playing; the local ranking remains available.
  • You can manage personalized ad permissions from your Google account's ad settings (adssettings.google.com).
  • When applicable, you can review and change your advertising consent choices in Chroma Blitz under Settings → Privacy options.
  • The app stores the age category, local ranking and audio/language preferences in its local app storage. Android may back up or restore some data depending on your device and account settings. Uninstalling does not itself delete information already sent to Google services; manage that information through the relevant Google account or service.
  • The age category and settings remain in the app while its data exists. The local ranking keeps at most the 50 highest scores. You can clear the app data in Android settings or uninstall the app; Android backup or restore settings may affect what returns, and Google Play Games may repopulate scores after sign-in. Google handles retention and deletion of information sent to its services under its own policies and account controls; no remote deletion period is promised here. See Google Play's data deletion guidance.

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or the services it uses change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
