Privacy Policy for RioAviso

Last updated: August 31, 2026

RioAviso is a river and reservoir monitoring app for Spain, developed by Alejandro Puerta (Alpuemed). This policy explains what data is collected when using the app and for what purpose.

1. Data we collect

Data stored on your device

  • Your favorites (marked rivers and reservoirs), your notification history, and your preferences (language, whether you have accepted the legal disclaimer, alerts enabled per station) are stored only in a local database on your device.
  • This data is not sent to any of our servers nor shared with third parties. It is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Station data the app queries

  • To show you river and reservoir levels, the app queries public services from official bodies (the SAIH networks of the Ebro, Tajo, Júcar, Segura, Duero, and Guadalquivir basins; ROAG and ACA in Catalonia; the Miño-Sil and Cantábrico river basin authorities; embalses.net; among others).
  • These requests do not include any data that identifies you personally or your location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • RioAviso shows ads (banners, native ads, interstitials, and rewarded ads) managed by Google AdMob.
  • Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, technical device information, an approximate location (based on IP), and ad interaction data, in order to show and measure ads.
  • More info: how Google uses data when apps use Google's services.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, in accordance with applicable regulations.

Notifications

  • The app periodically checks (roughly every 30 minutes) the status of the stations you follow and generates notifications locally on your device when it detects relevant changes.
  • This process happens entirely on your device; no data is sent to any of our servers to generate notifications.

RioAviso has no account system of its own and does not directly ask you for your name, email address, or any other contact information.

2. Children

RioAviso is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what is described in this policy (which applies equally to all users).

3. Your choices

  • You can disable alerts for a specific station from its detail screen, without affecting the rest of the app.
  • You can manage personalized ad permissions from your Google account's ad settings (adssettings.google.com).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally saved data (favorites, notifications, preferences).

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or the services it uses change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
