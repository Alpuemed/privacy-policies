RioAviso Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

RioAviso is an Android app providing information about rivers and reservoirs in Spain (package com.alpuemed.rioaviso), developed by Alejandro Puerta (Alpuemed). This policy explains what information may be processed when you use it and why. The app has no user account system or own server.

1. Data stored on your device

Your favorites, locally collected readings and history, notifications (up to 30 in total), and app preferences are stored locally. Reading history older than 30 days is cleaned up when the periodic background check runs; cleanup may be delayed. The latest-reading cache may be retained for longer. The app does not send your favorites to its servers; it operates no servers of its own.

Cloud backups configured by RioAviso include shared preferences, excluding device-specific preferences and the local database. Device-to-device transfers may also include those data, depending on Android and the manufacturer. You can clear local data in Android settings; backups and transfers depend on system settings and may restore data after reinstallation.

2. Requests to hydrological data providers

To display stations, levels, and related information, the app requests data from external hydrological agencies and providers. As with network requests generally, those providers and network infrastructure may receive your IP address and technical information needed to handle the request (such as protocol and device information). The app does not request or transmit your GPS location in these requests, and they do not include your favorites. External providers process data under their own policies.

3. Advertising and consent

Advertising is disabled by default in the app's current configuration until the advertising service is configured for a release. If enabled, RioAviso supports banner, native, and rewarded ads served through Google AdMob; no interstitial ads are active. Ad requests are made only when Google UMP consent status allows them.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, UMP may present consent options as applicable. Under Settings > Ad privacy, you can manage privacy choices when available, withdraw or change consent, or retry loading the options after an error. Depending on your choice, only limited ads may be available or no ads may be requested. Opening the privacy options removes loaded ads. Subsequent requests depend on the updated UMP status, which may allow limited ads after you reject personalization. This does not guarantee that all SDK traffic stops or control the processing of requests already sent.

When providing its services, Google and its providers may process IP address and approximate location derived from it, advertising identifiers and app set IDs, device information, app and ad activity and interactions (such as launches, taps, and video views), and diagnostic data to serve or measure ads and prevent fraud. See Google's AdMob data disclosure for Google Play, the Google Privacy Policy, and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Third parties' practices and retention periods are governed by their own policies; RioAviso does not determine or promise those periods.

Alejandro Puerta (Alpuemed) is responsible for operating RioAviso and configuring its privacy settings. Advertising and access to identifiers rely on your consent where required; UMP options allow you to change it. Google and other providers describe in their policies the legal bases, recipients, international transfers, and safeguards applicable to their processing.

4. Local alerts and notifications

The app schedules station checks approximately every 30 minutes through Android. Runs and delivery may be delayed or prevented by network availability, battery, power-saving settings, or operating-system decisions. Alerts are generated locally from the requests; favorites are not sent to a RioAviso server. Visible notifications also depend on your device permissions and settings.

5. Children

RioAviso is not specifically directed at children. Third-party services may process data under their own terms and policies.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing, or data portability, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Locally stored data can be managed in the app or device settings; the developer cannot access it remotely. To exercise rights regarding processing for which the developer is responsible, contact us below. For processing by Google or another provider, contact that provider. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency, without prejudice to contacting the competent authority where you live.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app or its data practices change; the date above identifies the current revision.
