RioAviso privacy policy

This draft describes how the RioAviso Android app handles information. The open points need a decision by the controller before publication. The controller may consult legal counsel.

Who is responsible

The controller is Alejandro Puerta Medina (Alpuemed). Contact: alejandro.puerta.medina@gmail.com. App identifier: com.alpuemed.rioaviso. RioAviso has no user accounts and no backend of its own. The app does not request GPS location.

Information stored on your device

The app stores favorites, hydrological readings used by the app, and notification history in a local Room database. It also stores app preferences, including advertising privacy choices managed by Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app keeps up to 30 notifications in total. A background worker removes local readings older than 30 days; removal can be delayed if the system does not run the worker. The latest cached values may remain longer. These local records are not sent to a RioAviso server because RioAviso has no such server.

Cloud backup includes shared preferences except device_preferences.xml. The Room database is excluded from this cloud-backup configuration. On Android 12 and later, the device-transfer rules currently restrict cloud backup only; a device-to-device transfer may include the database. Android, the device maker, and the backup or transfer service control their own processes and settings. Their behavior can vary. You can use Android backup and transfer controls to manage this.

Network requests and hydrological data

To load station lists and water data, the app contacts the relevant hydrological data providers. Those providers and network operators can receive your IP address and technical request information. A request can include a station code. We therefore do not claim that a saved favorite can never be inferred from network traffic. RioAviso does not keep app-server request logs because it has no app server.

Depending on the station and basin, requests can go to data services associated with Guadalquivir, Red Hidrosur, ACA, Cantábrico, Duero, Ebro, Tajo, Júcar, Segura, Miño-Sil, MeteoGalicia, or ROAG. These names identify the services or data sources used by the app; this notice does not assign a legal entity to each name.

The app uses a PHP session cookie named PHPSESSID in memory for a technical session with the Miño-Sil provider. The app does not save that cookie to its own logs.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app includes Google Mobile Ads SDK 23.6.0 and UMP SDK 4.0.0. Ads are disabled by default in the current app configuration. If enabled in a future release, the only ad placement currently connected in the app interface is a banner on a station detail screen. Native-ad and rewarded-ad helper code is not used. The app has no interstitial ad placement.

Before an ad request is allowed, the app asks UMP to update its status each time the app returns to the foreground. The ad gate stays closed if that update fails. Settings provides controls to review or manage ad privacy choices, withdraw a choice where UMP offers that control, or retry a failed update. Refusing personalized advertising may still allow other ad requests. The exact ad mode served after each choice has not been independently verified. We cannot promise that the advertising SDK makes no network contact or sends no data when personalized ads are refused.

Google's advertising SDK may process an IP address that can indicate approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or advertising identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID) or App Set ID, as described in the provider's disclosures. This is a description of possible provider processing. We have not verified that every listed field is collected in every session. Current provider documents may describe SDK versions newer than the version used by RioAviso.

RioAviso's AdMob controls currently set a maximum ad content rating of T (Teen) and block selected sensitive categories. These filters apply to all users, including adults. They do not identify a user's age or provide separate advertising for adults and teenagers.

Logs and diagnostics

RioAviso writes fixed, general app events to the Android system log buffer. It does not write an app log file. The advertising SDK may have separate diagnostics and data handling under the provider's control. Google provider documentation currently describes SDK version 25.5.0, while RioAviso includes version 23.6.0; the newer documentation may not describe the installed version exactly.

Purposes and proposed legal bases

The app uses local information to provide requested features such as favorites, current readings, and notifications. The proposed legal basis is performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where that basis applies. The controller must review this proposed basis before publication and may consult legal counsel.

Optional advertising and access to or storage of information on a device may rely on consent where required, including under Article 22(2) of Spain's LSSI. Provider security processing may rely on legitimate interests, subject to review by the controller before publication. These are proposed bases, not a conclusion that all processing is compliant. The controller must confirm the basis, roles, notices, and consent flow before publication.

Recipients and international processing

Hydrological data providers receive requests needed to return their data. Google provides UMP and advertising services. Google may process information in global infrastructure, including outside the European Economic Area. Google describes transfer safeguards at Google's data transfer framework information. The exact service roles, processing regions, and retention periods have not been independently verified. No retention period is stated here for those providers.

Google describes standard AdMob as a service covered by its Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms, except for listed processor features. Those terms describe Google and the customer as independent controllers only when agreed for the relevant service. Google lists some AdMob features, including Secure Signals and server-side impression-level ad revenue, as processor services in its service information. RioAviso has not verified the account-specific agreement or enabled features. UMP roles have not been verified; this AdMob role description is not assumed to apply to UMP.

How long information is kept

Favorites and preferences remain on the device until you remove or reset them, uninstall the app, or Android removes app data, subject to backup and device-transfer behavior described above. Local readings older than 30 days are removed when the worker runs; the latest cached values may remain longer. Notification history is limited to the 30 most recent notifications in total. Google says its retention depends on data type and purpose, user settings, and security or legal needs. Its general retention information does not establish a period for the services RioAviso uses. Provider-specific periods have not been verified. No single provider retention period is promised.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability where those rights apply. Send a free request to the controller at alejandro.puerta.medina@gmail.com. Use the subject "RioAviso - data deletion request" and describe what you request. Do not send a password or your full advertising ID. When you email, your message and sender details are processed by the controller and the email service used to send or receive it. The controller remains responsible for handling the request; you are not required to contact a provider instead. A response is generally due within one month. That period may be extended by up to two further months for complex or numerous requests, with notice and reasons within the first month.

RioAviso has no user accounts or backend through which it can access or remotely delete your local database. Remove favorites in the app or clear RioAviso storage in Android settings to remove local data. This does not automatically delete Android backup or transfer copies, or records held by independent providers. The controller handles requests sent to RioAviso. This notice does not promise remote deletion of provider records; their own deletion options and retention terms apply.

Proposed email retention procedure for approval; not yet adopted: Support messages are kept while the enquiry is handled and deleted within 90 days after it is resolved, unless a legal obligation or a specific dispute requires keeping the necessary evidence for longer. Rights requests retain only the evidence needed to handle the request and demonstrate compliance for the applicable legal period. The controller must approve this proposal before publication.

You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). You may also withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not make processing carried out before withdrawal unlawful.

Children and teenagers

The saved Play Console target audience is 16–17 and 18 and over. The change has not been submitted for Google review, and the store listing is not yet published. The app does not verify age, restrict access by age, or provide a parental-consent flow. Declaring a target audience does not establish a user's actual age. Under Spanish law, a child under 14 needs a parent or guardian's consent when consent is the legal basis for processing. The intended age scope, applicable legal bases, notices, and any safeguards need human review before release.

Changes to this notice

The controller should update this notice when app behavior or processing changes and show the effective date in any published version. This draft is dated 21 October 2026 and is not in force as a published notice.

Controller review before publication

Before publication, the controller must confirm the controller and contact details; purposes and legal bases; the role of each provider; the scope and wording of age-related notices; device backup disclosures; transfer safeguards; retention details available from providers; and whether the consent and advertising descriptions match the release build and live configuration. The controller may consult legal counsel. No blanket compliance statement is made here.
